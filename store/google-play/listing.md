## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

### EN

Flourish is a private, local-first money manager. Your accounts,
transactions, budgets and goals are stored on your phone. There is no
sign-up, no ads and no Flourish server.

TRACK EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Bank, cash, wallet and credit-card accounts in many currencies
• Income, expenses and transfers, with categories, merchants and notes
• A monthly view of income, spending and what you saved
• Net worth, with reference exchange rates for multi-currency totals

PLAN AHEAD
• Monthly, weekly or yearly budgets per category
• Savings goals with progress and deadlines
• Recurring bills and salaries, plus payment reminders
• Credit cards: limits, statements, minimum due and interest estimates
• Holdings, gold, crypto and loans in a Wealth view

CATCH PAYMENTS AUTOMATICALLY (ANDROID)
• With your permission, Flourish reads bank SMS and the bank or wallet app
  alerts you choose, in English and Arabic, and turns them into a review
  inbox
• One-time codes and declined payments are ignored; card numbers are cut to
  the last 4 digits
• You approve each payment, unless you turn on automatic adding

IMPORT AND EXPORT
• Import CSV, Excel, OFX, QIF and PDF bank statements, with a review step
  before anything is saved
• Share an alert or screenshot into Flourish to add it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Records are stored only on your phone and are left out of cloud backup
• Password-encrypted backups you control, for safekeeping or a new phone
• Daily on-device backups are encrypted
• Fingerprint, face or passcode app lock
• No ads, no analytics, no account

OPTIONAL AI ASSISTANT
Off by default. Bring your own API key for Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq,
OpenRouter or a compatible server to read alerts and statements Flourish
can't read on its own.
• Nothing is sent unless you tap "Ask AI", or separately agree to automatic
  reading for unclear alerts
• Text goes directly from your phone to the provider you chose
• Account and card numbers, phones, emails and codes are masked first

Flourish is a tracking tool. It does not move money, connect to your bank
or give financial advice.

تابع كل شيء في مكان واحد

التقاط المدفوعات تلقائيًا (أندرويد)
• بإذنك، يقرأ Flourish رسائل البنك القصيرة وتنبيهات تطبيقات البنوك أو
  المحافظ التي تختارها، بالعربية والإنجليزية، ويحوّلها إلى صندوق مراجعة

مساعد ذكاء اصطناعي اختياري
متوقف افتراضيًا. استخدم مفتاح API الخاص بك لـ Google Gemini أو DeepSeek أو
