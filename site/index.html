Everything in one calm view

Bank alerts → payments

On Android, Flourish reads the bank and wallet alerts you choose, in Arabic and English, and fills in amount, shop and category. You approve each one.

Budgets that help

Set a limit per category and see it fill as you spend, before the month runs out.

See where it goes

Reports by category and month, across cash, bank, cards, wallets and currencies.

Goals and net worth

Savings goals, recurring bills, credit cards, holdings and loans in one place.

Import what you have

CSV, Excel, OFX, QIF and PDF statements, with a review step before anything is saved.

Arabic and English

The whole app, both ways, made for EGP and local banks and wallets.

Private by design

  • No sign-up, no ads, no selling data.
  • Your records stay on your phone and are left out of cloud backups.
  • Daily backups are encrypted on your phone. Moving phones? Export a password-protected backup.
  • The AI helper is off by default. If you turn it on, only messages you choose (or unclear alerts, if you agree) go, masked, to the provider you pick.
